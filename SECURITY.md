# Security

Frame Tidy handles an Aura sign-in and personal photos, and can open a page
to your home wifi when you turn on "Use on my phone". Please report security
problems privately, not in a public issue.

**How to report:** on GitHub, open the repository's **Security** tab and choose
**Report a vulnerability**. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and
what someone could do with it. You will get a reply there.

What is in scope: anything in this repository or in the app downloads from its
Releases page. Problems in Aura's own service should go to Aura.
